Policy Section,Sample Text to Include
Annual Leave (Vacation),"Policy: All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Employees must submit a request via the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused leave can be rolled over, up to a maximum of 5 days."
Sick Leave,"Policy: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences exceeding three consecutive days, a doctor's note must be provided to the HR department. Sick leave does not roll over to the next year."
Work From Home (WFH),"Policy: Flexible work arrangements, including WFH, are permitted two days per week, subject to manager approval and team scheduling needs. Employees must maintain adequate internet connection and be available during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM IST)."
Performance Review,"Policy: Formal performance reviews are conducted semi-annually, in June and December. Goals are set in January. Raises and bonuses are typically determined following the December review."
